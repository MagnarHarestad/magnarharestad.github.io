Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

MagMail is a desktop e-mail application for Windows that connects to your Gmail account. It is developed by Magnar Harestad (Censor Design). This policy explains what data MagMail accesses, how it is used and where it is stored.

In short: MagMail runs entirely on your own computer. There is no MagMail server, account, analytics or advertising. Your mail goes only between your PC and Google, and to Anthropic only when you use a Claude feature.

Data MagMail accesses

With your permission, granted through Google's own sign-in page, MagMail uses the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to:

  • read your messages, threads and labels so they can be shown in the app;
  • change labels, archive, mark read/unread, star and move messages to Trash when you ask it to;
  • create drafts and send messages that you write and choose to send.

MagMail cannot permanently delete mail, and it never sees or stores your Google password.

Where your data is stored

  • On your computer only. Your OAuth access token, cached messages and settings are stored in your Windows user profile. The token and cached mail are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account on that PC can read them.
  • If you turn on the optional "local folders" feature, copies of messages from the labels you choose are saved as .eml files in a folder you pick. These can optionally be encrypted.
  • The developer does not operate any server and does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

Sharing with third parties

  • Google: MagMail communicates directly with Google's Gmail API over HTTPS to provide the app's features.
  • Anthropic (optional): if you add your own Anthropic API key and use a Claude feature (the Claude chat panel, "Summarize" or "Write"), the relevant e-mail content and your request are sent directly from your PC to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) to generate the answer. This only happens when you use one of these features. Anthropic processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • No data is sold, shared for advertising, or transferred to anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MagMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the user-facing features of the app that you use. It is not used for advertising, is not read by humans, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Your control

  • Sign out in MagMail's Settings revokes MagMail's access at Google and deletes the token and the local mail cache from your PC.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling MagMail and deleting %APPDATA%\MagMail and %LOCALAPPDATA%\MagMail removes all of its data.

Children

MagMail is not directed at children under 13.
