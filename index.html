MagMail

A modern, security-focused Gmail client for Windows, with an AI assistant built in to help sort, find, summarize and draft your mail. It runs free and private on your own PC, or on Claude.

Download MagMail 1.1.0

💬

Conversations, not lists

Threads read top to bottom with a timeline of who wrote when. Replies open right under the message you're answering.

🏷️

Labels as folders

Your Gmail labels as a folder tree, with drag-and-drop sorting. Labels can also be mirrored to local folders on your PC.

✨

AI assistant

Find what needs a reply, sort your inbox, summarize threads, draft answers and write reports. It runs on a free local model (Ollama, mail never leaves your PC) or on Claude, always asks before changing anything, and can never send mail.

🔒

Private by design

Sign in with Google OAuth, never a password. Tokens and cached mail are encrypted on your PC, and there is no MagMail server.
